#AgentArchitect

Engineering Agent Platform for Civil Engineers Specialized in Fire Safety
AgentArchitect is a desktop platform that helps civil engineering teams transition from organized prompt usage to AI agents — with a vertical focus on fire safety review against the Romanian P118 standard.
Built during a hackathon focused on AI adoption in engineering. Theme: "Apps that encourage AI adoption".
